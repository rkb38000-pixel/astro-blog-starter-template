# Hyundai Palisade LX3 Bose Audio Upgrade

Today we introduce an audio upgrade for the new Hyundai Palisade LX3 equipped with the factory Bose audio system.

The Bose system has strong bass, but the high frequencies can feel limited. This can make the sound less clear and prevent the soundstage from opening up across the dashboard.

## DSP Amplifier Upgrade

We installed and tuned a DSP amplifier while keeping the factory audio system.

The goal was to improve:

- High-frequency clarity
- Vocal definition
- Bass response
- Soundstage width
- Overall balance
